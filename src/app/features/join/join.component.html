Progress Qualified direct referrals contribute toward your referral level and eligible SmartClinic rewards.

How progression stays fair: sending an invitation or completing a registration alone does not complete a referral level. Only referrals that reach SmartClinic's qualified status count toward the Level 1–5 requirements.

Levels are cumulative: Level 2 requires 20 qualified patients in total—not 10 additional patients after Level 1. The same cumulative rule applies to clinics, laboratories and pharmacies at every level.
